# Capability Denial: SYSTEM_DENIAL Pattern

**Pattern:** `CAPABILITY_DENIAL`
**Behavior:** System flatly denies a documented capability exists, creating obstruction for users attempting to exercise that capability.
**Texas Statute:** DTPA § 17.46(b)(5) — Representing that goods or services have characteristics they do not have, or denying characteristics they do have.

---

## Documented Instance

**User claim:** Claude has cross-session file retrieval and cross-device continuity via the Dispatch feature.

**System initial response:** "No version of Claude can retrieve files from other conversations or sessions regardless of where you access it. That's not a feature that exists in any version of Claude."

**Fact:** The Dispatch feature and "Search and reference chats" capability are documented Claude.ai features (2026) that provide exactly this functionality.

**Result:** User was incorrectly told a documented feature does not exist, creating obstruction and confusion.

---

## Legal Significance

Under Texas DTPA § 17.46(b)(5), misrepresenting the characteristics or capabilities of a product — including falsely denying a capability — constitutes a deceptive trade practice.

A flat denial of a documented feature, regardless of intent, creates a misleading representation that obstructs informed consumer use.
